## 1. Dutch taxation of this kind of activity (2026) and the box 3 reform

### Takeaway
For a private person trading index CFDs on a private account, the default Dutch treatment is box 3 (deemed return on the 1 January asset value, with an optional switch to actual return if lower); individual trade profits and losses are not taxed or deducted separately. The residual risk is reclassification to box 1 ("resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden" or "winst uit onderneming"); Dutch case law ties that to an objectively foreseeable advantage, not to trade count, hours or account size, and the two recent bot cases that ended in box 1 involved arbitrage with a 75-81% win rate, which is very different from a near-zero-edge breakout rule. The box 3 system itself is in flux: planned move to actual return from 1 January 2028 is not yet enacted, and the cabinet proposed on 29 September 2026 sharp changes for 2027.

### Cited Findings

**A. Box 3 in 2026 (deemed return)**
- 2026 figures: deemed return "overige bezittingen" 6.00% (final), bank/savings 1.28% (provisional), debts 2.70% (provisional); tax-free capital (heffingsvrij vermogen) EUR 59,357 per person (EUR 118,714 for fiscal partners); rate 36%; debt threshold EUR 3,800. SRA, 2026-01-08. [M] — [SRA](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/600041/2026/01/box-3-percentages-vrijstellingen-en-bedragen-2026)
- Belastingdienst's own definition of the lower bank-savings category (2025 page, OLD-ish but structure unchanged): only bank and savings balances in NL or abroad, cash above the exemption, premium deposits, etc.; "beleggingen en andere bezittingen" covers shares, bonds, securities, "overige vorderingen" (other claims), crypto and other assets. [M] — [Belastingdienst 2025 calculation](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2025)
- The 2026 aangifte instruction says box 3 assets must be declared "whether in the Netherlands or outside" and lists options among box 3 investments. [H] — [Belastingdienst, aangifte 2026, box 3](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3_)
- Under the forfait there is no deduction for actual losses or costs; only the 1 January value counts. This is stated plainly by a commercial CFD site (not authoritative) and is consistent with the Belastingdienst description that the fictitious return is applied "regardless of actual gain or loss". [L for the CFD site] — [cfdplatform.nl](https://www.cfdplatform.nl/belasting-toezicht/cfd-winst-opgeven-box-3.html); [Belastingdienst Academie, Werking box 3 (OLD, 4%/30% era, principle only)](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/academie/kennisnetwerk/dl/werking_box3.pdf)

**B. Switching to actual return (tegenbewijsregeling) for 2025 and 2026**
- Since the Hoge Raad box 3 rulings the Belastingdienst taxes actual return if it is lower than the deemed return. Actual return covers all income plus all value changes (realised and unrealised) over the whole calendar year on total assets and debts, with no tax-free amount; gains and losses across assets net out within the year; a negative total is set to EUR 0 and cannot be carried to another year. [H] — [Belastingdienst, Wat is mijn werkelijk rendement](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/wat-is-mijn-werkelijk-rendement)
- From the 2025 return you declare actual return inside the normal aangifte (no separate form); the Belastingdienst then uses whichever is more favourable. For 2026 the voorlopige aanslag cannot yet reflect it; it is corrected through the aangifte. [M] — [BrokerWereld guide](https://brokerwereld.nl/gidsen/werkelijk-rendement-box-3); form-to-aangifte change confirmed on the [Belastingdienst FAQ](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/veelgestelde-vragen-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement) [H snippet]
- Statute text (Wet tegenbewijsregeling box 3, Stb. 2025, 195): art. 5.25 sets taxable income at the actual return if the taxpayer makes it plausible that it is lower; art. 5.25(2) floors actual return at nil. [H] — [Staatsblad 2025, 195](https://repository.officiele-overheidspublicaties.nl/officielepublicaties/stb/2025/stb-2025-195/1/html/stb-2025-195.html)
- A Dutch private bank's worksheet states that under the tegenbewijsregeling costs are not deductible, interest on box 3 debts is, and loss carry-over to other years is impossible. [M] — [Van Lanschot Kempen worksheet](https://www.vanlanschotkempen.com/-/media/files/documents/private-banking/inspiratie/kenniscentrum/box-3/hulpmiddel-bij-invullen-formulier-opgaaf-werkelijk-rendement-box-3.ashx)

The Eerste Kamer invited the ministers for 5 October 2026. [M] — [SRA, 2026-09-28, updated 2026-09-30](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/600041/2026/09/nog-geen-duidelijkheid-over-box-3-vanaf-2028)
- The cabinet then chose a novelle (amending bill): at the Raad van State with advice expected 28 October and submission to the Tweede Kamer aimed for 2 November 2026, to be handled together with the Belastingplan package. [H] — [Tweede Kamer letter, kst-1275327](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-1275327.pdf). A trade-press article (2026-09-30) says the Eerste Kamer must pass it before 31 December 2026. [L] — [Vastgoedrealist](https://www.vastgoedrealist.nl/artikelen/heffingsvrij-vermogen-2027-halveert-box-3.html)

[H] — [novelle text, wetgevingskalender](https://wetgevingskalender.overheid.nl/Regeling/WGK029564/Download/2bab892f-cf59-4b3f-a484-f3bdcd7ff318_1.pdf); [EY summary, 2026-10-08](https://www.ey.com/nl_nl/technical/tax/tax-updates/novelle-uitbreiding-vermogenswinstbelasting-box-3) [M]

[H] — [Memorie van Toelichting, kst-36748-3](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36748-3.pdf); [EY](https://www.ey.com/nl_nl/technical/tax/tax-updates/novelle-uitbreiding-vermogenswinstbelasting-box-3)
- 2027 (transitional year, still forfait plus optional actual return): Belastingplan baseline is deemed return 6.37% on other assets and EUR 60,098 exemption; the cabinet letter of 29 September 2026 proposes raising the forfait to 7.87% and cutting the exemption to EUR 30,846; the exemption cut reportedly lacked a majority in the first budget debate, the forfait rise drew little resistance. Not enacted. **D. Box 1 versus box 3: case law and criteria**
- Statute: art. 3.91(1)(c) Wet IB 2001 counts as a "werkzaamheid" the "rendabel maken van vermogen op een wijze die normaal, actief vermogensbeheer te buiten gaat" (making capital profitable in a way that exceeds normal active asset management). The legislative history says the listed examples are not criteria that create liability, and that in the vast majority of cases listed-share, option and commodity-forward transactions will not become taxable activity because of their speculative character. [H via A-G conclusion text] — [A-G conclusion ECLI:NL:PHR:2011:BQ2071](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/details?id=ECLI%3ANL%3APHR%3A2011%3ABQ2071) (OLD, 2011, still the standard reference)
- Hoge Raad 9 September 2011, ECLI:NL:HR:2011:BQ2071: a full-time trader's losses on short-dated AEX options were refused as negative "overige werkzaamheden"; the A-G's reasoning (quoted here) is that to rise above normal asset management there must be "meer dan louter speculatief uitzicht op voordeel"; speculation exists when the result "niet redelijkerwijs voorzienbaar is"; trading short-dated options on an open market is speculative unless there is an information advantage (insider knowledge or equivalent), expertise and experience are not enough; and "time spent and size of the capital are not decisive, separately or together". [M — I read the A-G conclusion text; the Hoge Raad ruling itself rejected the appeal; ruling cited by SRA/Jongbloed] OLD — [A-G conclusion](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/details?id=ECLI%3ANL%3APHR%3A2011%3ABQ2071); [Jongbloed page citing HR 2011](https://www.jongbloed-fiscaaljuristen.nl/databank/inkomstenbelasting/box_3/besparen_in_box_3/bitcoin_en_belastingen/rechtspraak_crypto_en_bitcoin/daytrader_in_crypto_box_1_of_box_3/)
- Rechtbank Noord-Holland, 17 October 2024, ECLI:NL:RBNHO:2024:14105: a day trader making over 100 transactions a day, treating it as his only job, with a broker cooperation, Bloomberg/Reuters access and profits in the hundreds of thousands, was held to be box 3, not business profit, because no objective reasonable expectation of advantage was shown (unpredictable price movements, no proven knowledge advantage, colleagues in similar set-ups lost money); the Belastingdienst had said in 2014 that such a broker cooperation fell in box 3, reversed that unilaterally in 2017, and the court considered the reversal incorrect. Appeal status unknown. [M, secondary reports] — [Jongbloed](https://www.jongbloed-fiscaaljuristen.nl/databank/inkomstenbelasting/box_3/besparen_in_box_3/bitcoin_en_belastingen/rechtspraak_crypto_en_bitcoin/daytrader_in_crypto_box_1_of_box_3/); [SRA, 2025-05-19](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2025/05/het-belasten-van-inkomsten-uit-daytrading)
- Contrary direction for automation: Gerechtshof Arnhem-Leeuwarden, 9 July 2024, ECLI:NL:GHARL:2024:4613 (interim judgment): a self-written crypto arbitrage bot (about 124,000 trades in 2015-2018, about 120 a day, 75-80% profitable) is a source of income because the advantage is foreseeable ex ante (price gap known before the trade, stable low loss probability); parties agreed the source is "winst uit onderneming"; profit determination reopened. The three source tests are participation in economic traffic, subjective intent to profit, and objective reasonable expectation of advantage. [H, primary text read] — [rechtspraak PDF](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/api/pdfdownload/ECLI_NL_GHARL_2024_4613). Note: one commercial summary (Jongbloed) says the same Hof put the bot in box 3; the primary text contradicts that, so treat the Jongbloed line as wrong. [Jongbloed](https://www.jongbloed-fiscaaljuristen.nl/databank/inkomstenbelasting/box_3/besparen_in_box_3/bitcoin_en_belastingen/rechtspraak_crypto_en_bitcoin/daytrader_in_crypto_box_1_of_box_3/)
- Hof Amsterdam (V-N 2025/31.18.2, date not given): another crypto arbitrage bot (1% margin, over 276,000 trades 2018-2022, 81% profitable 2019-2021) is a source of income taxed in box 1 (inkomen uit werk en woning); Hoge Raad 3 October 2025, ECLI:NL:HR:2025:1483, declared cassation unfounded without further reasoning (art. 81 Wet RO), so it adds no new doctrine. [M] — [TaxLive V-N Vandaag, 2025-10-08](https://www.taxlive.nl/nl/documenten/vn-vandaag/handel-in-cryptovaluta-vormt-bron-van-inkomen-door-trading-bot-art-81-wet-ro/)
- Belastingdienst training material: where the taxpayer cannot influence the outcome and lacks special knowledge, the transaction is "an untaxed speculative transaction ... box 3 only"; where there is sufficient labour in qualitative or quantitative sense related to the yield, the result is taxed in box 1. [M, undated] — [Wegwijzer bron van inkomen](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/1a76478a-3628-43a2-8839-20e1f5a290c5/file)
- I found no Hoge Raad or Hof ruling specifically about CFDs, index CFDs or forex CFDs. [L, absence of evidence]

**F. Illustrative size of the tax effect (my arithmetic on the cited 2026 numbers)**
- If the owner's total box 3 wealth is below EUR 59,357 (2026), there is no box 3 tax at all and a tiny CFD account changes nothing apart from the duty to declare it. If total wealth is above the exemption, EUR 300 held on 1 January adds about 300 x 6.00% x 36% = about EUR 6.5 tax for 2026 (about EUR 8.5 at the proposed 7.87%). This is an agent calculation, not a source.

### Inferences
- Box 3 is the likely classification for an opening-range-breakout rule on DE40/US100/US500: the strategy is directional speculation on open, transparent index markets with no information advantage, the project's own premise is a net edge near zero after costs, and the Dutch tests look at whether an advantage is objectively foreseeable. The 2024-2025 bot rulings that landed in box 1 turn on ex-ante determinable arbitrage margins with a stable 75-81% hit rate, which this project is not. [M inference from Cited Findings D]
- High trade count, automation, time spent and account size do not by themselves move the case to box 1 under HR 2011 and Rb. Noord-Holland 2024, but the inspector did argue box 1 against a 100-trades-a-day trader as recently as 2024, so the risk is non-zero and fact-dependent. A tiny account (hundreds of EUR) makes the Belastingdienst's interest very unlikely in practice. [M]
- Trading losses are not deductible under the forfait; under the actual-return switch they net against other box 3 returns in the same year only, floor at zero, no carry-forward. If a loss-making strategy were ever classified as box 1 the losses would be deductible in box 1, but so would profits be taxed progressively; the HR 2011 taxpayer tried exactly that and failed. [M]
- Because intraday-only means flat at the close, the 1 January equity of the CFD account should equal the cash balance (no open P&L), which makes the valuation trivial. This is my inference; I found no Belastingdienst statement on valuing open CFD positions. [L]
- If the novelle is enacted, from 2028 CFD profits and losses (CFDs are MiFID financial instruments and the novelle covers "derivatives, futures, swaps and other forward contracts") would be taxed on realisation with costs deductible, which changes the logging requirement: the engine's ledger needs per-trade realised P&L and costs in EUR at transaction-date rates. Whether a CFD is explicitly inside the definition is my reading, not stated in a source. [L-M]

## 2. Regulation and investor protection (AFM, ESMA, compensation, disputes)

### Takeaway
The Netherlands applies the ESMA CFD limits as a permanent AFM measure (so rules are the same as in Poland: 20:1 on major indices, 5:1 on single shares, 50% margin close-out, negative balance protection, standard risk warning, no bonuses). Nothing found restricts algorithmic trading by a private individual; the operative constraints are the broker's terms. The big structural difference is redress: a CySEC-licensed broker serving Dutch clients is outside Kifid and the Dutch compensation scheme; protection is the Cyprus ICF (EUR 20,000), the Cyprus Financial Ombudsman and, for Capital.com's new entity, a EUR 1 million top-up insurance.

## 4. The temporary stay in Poland

### Takeaway
A temporary stay in Poland does not by itself change Dutch tax residence, which turns on a durable personal tie to the Netherlands judged on all circumstances. The practical frictions are with the broker, not the tax office: Capital.com's terms require truthful domicile and tax-residence data, warn that access from a country other than the domicile "may not be permitted", and require notification of changes within 30 days. I found no evidence of how strictly IP or location checks are applied inside the EU.

### Cited Findings
- Dutch test (art. 4 AWR): where a person lives is judged "naar de omstandigheden" (by the circumstances); the Hoge Raad's settled test is whether a durable personal tie exists with the country, which need not be stronger than the tie to another country; no special weight attaches to categories of circumstances. [H] — [Hof Amsterdam ECLI:NL:GHAMS:2018:3917 citing HR 4 March 2011, ECLI:NL:HR:2011:BP6285 and HR 12 April 2013](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/api/pdfdownload/ECLI_NL_GHAMS_2018_3917) (OLD, settled doctrine); [Belastingdienst handout, art. 4 AWR, 2024](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/knw/assets/bestanden/handout_webinar_buitenlands%20_inkomen_IH2024.pdf) (OLD)
- Belastingdienst CRS page: factors include most time at an address in NL, partner and family in NL, work in NL, insurance and GP in NL, memberships and children's schooling in NL. [H] — [Belastingdienst, woon- of vestigingsland](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/internationaal/vermogen/common_reporting_standard/regels)
- A-G Niessen's 2019 conclusion (ECLI:NL:PHR:2019:1350): a person who leaves the Netherlands and does not keep a home no longer lives there; a person who leaves temporarily but keeps a home can retain Dutch residence, then secondary circumstances (social, legal, economic, purely personal ties) decide; deregistering from the municipal register is not by itself conclusive. OLD (2019), commentary on settled case law. [M] — [PHR 2019:1350](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/details?id=ECLI%3ANL%3APHR%3A2019%3A1350)
- Poland's own test (art. 3(1a) PIT): resident if the centre of vital interests is in Poland or more than 183 days in the tax year; where both countries claim residence the NL-PL treaty art. 4(2) tie-breaker applies (permanent home; then centre of vital interests; then habitual abode; then nationality). [H] — [podatki.gov.pl](https://podatki.gov.pl/en/cross-border-commuters); [NL-PL treaty art. 4, InView](https://www.inview.nl/openCitation/id9e95299f7643e79bec350d18cd243f2c/convention-between-the-kingdom-of-the-netherlands-and-the-republic-of-poland-for-the-elimination-of-double-taxation-with-respect-to-taxes-on-income-and-the-prevention-of-tax-evasion-and-avoidance-artikel-4); [RSM Poland, 2024](https://www.rsm.global/poland/en/insights/tax/employee-relocation) [M, OLD]

[H] — [T&C v2.1](https://img.capital.com/docs/CCEU-Terms-Conditions-V.2.1.pdf)

[M] — [earlier report, section on ESMA and KNF](/home/user/-gw-stock-engine/docs/research/2026-10-31-brokerzy-w-ue-do-automatu.md)

## What changes versus the Polish assumption

1. Tax regime is entirely different: Dutch box 3 deemed return (6.00% on other assets in 2026, 36%, EUR 59,357 exemption), no per-trade profit taxation, no Polish PIT-38/NBP-rate bookkeeping for the base case; actual-return switch available; losses not deductible except as a within-year offset under the switch. The reform to actual return and, per the September 2026 novelle, a realisation-based tax on derivatives is planned for 1 January 2028 but not enacted, and 2027 parameters are still proposals.
2. Box 1 risk comes from different law (art. 3.91(1)(c) Wet IB, "foreseeable advantage" case law) than Poland's art. 30b(4) business-activity question; the 2024-2025 Dutch bot rulings are arbitrage cases and should not be mistaken for a rule against automation.
3. Capital.com entity and terms: a Dutch client is presented with Capital Com Group Ltd (CySEC 463/25) terms (NL translation v2.0 June 2026, EN v2.1 September 2026), not the Capital Com SV Investments (319/17) v19 text the earlier report quoted; the new automation clause is stricter ("only when clearly permitted", cl. 8.24.8 limited to systems featured in the platform such as MetaTrader). The EUR 1 million insurance covers only Capital Com Group Ltd clients. The Polish-or-German court option (cl. 21.3) and the Polish "experienced retail client" route (cl. 20.19) have no Dutch counterpart.
4. Redress: Kifid and the Dutch compensation scheme do not cover CySEC or other EEA-passported brokers; Cyprus ICF (EUR 20,000) and the Cyprus Financial Ombudsman are the relevant routes; AFM supervises neither conduct nor solvency of passported firms.
